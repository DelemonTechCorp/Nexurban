01 — The Community

About Greenwood

Aldar Greenwood introduces a fresh vision of family living in Dubai Silicon Oasis, bringing together a green-community concept and a choice of homes designed for different stages of life — from a first family townhouse to a five-bedroom villa.

The community is planned around landscaped open space, so daily routines — the school run, an evening walk, weekend play — happen among trees and parks rather than traffic. It sits inside an established district with schools, retail and major road links already in place.

Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai

Greenwood is planned for buyers who want a green residential setting with the convenience of an established Dubai address.
