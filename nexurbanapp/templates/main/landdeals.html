FAQ

Land Buying Questions Answered

01

Can foreigners buy land in Dubai?

Yes — foreign nationals can acquire freehold land in designated investment zones across Dubai, subject to Dubai Land Department regulations. Nexurban confirms eligibility and zoning for every opportunity we present.

02

What should I check before buying land in Dubai?

Key checks include zoning and permitted use, title deed verification, plot dimensions and boundaries, infrastructure access, and any outstanding service charges or development conditions attached to the plot.

03

Is buying land a good investment compared to buying a built property in Dubai?

Land can offer higher long-term upside since value is unlocked through development or master-plan growth, but it also carries different risk — no immediate rental income and dependency on future zoning or infrastructure changes. It suits investors with a longer time horizon and development appetite.

04

What is the process for buying land in Dubai?

The process typically involves identifying an eligible plot, verifying title and zoning with the relevant land authority, agreeing terms, and completing registration and transfer through the Dubai Land Department. Nexurban coordinates each step on the buyer's behalf.

05

Can I develop the land myself after purchase, or do I need a partner?

You can develop independently if you have the capital and expertise, or structure a joint development or joint venture with an experienced developer — a common approach for landowners who prefer to share execution risk. Nexurban can facilitate either path.
