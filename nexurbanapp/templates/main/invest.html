FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Dubai real estate a good investment in 2026?

Yes — Dubai continues to record strong transaction volumes and rental demand, supported by population growth, no property tax and a stable regulatory environment. However, returns vary significantly by community, developer and entry price, which is why professional evaluation matters before you invest.

What is the minimum investment required to buy property in Dubai?

Off-plan units in emerging communities can start from around AED 400,000–600,000, while ready properties in established areas typically start higher. Nexurban helps match your budget to opportunities with genuine investment potential rather than just the lowest price point.

Can foreigners invest in Dubai real estate?

Yes. Dubai allows 100% freehold ownership for foreign nationals in designated investment zones, with no restriction on rental income repatriation, making it accessible for international investors.

What rental yields can I expect in Dubai?

Gross rental yields in Dubai typically range from 5–9% depending on community, property type and market cycle — generally higher than many global gateway cities. Nexurban provides realistic, data-backed yield projections rather than inflated marketing figures.

Does investing in Dubai property qualify me for a UAE Golden Visa?

Property investment above AED 2 million can qualify for the UAE's 10-year Golden Visa, subject to current immigration rules. Nexurban can guide you on structuring an investment that also supports visa eligibility.
